DAILY LOOP

A small routine you can repeat.

The goal is not to fill every minute. It is to shorten the distance from intention to action.

01

Scan the month, today, and this week

Review time commitments first, then check routines and work items that can fit around them.

02

Choose one next action

Select a specific schedule item, routine, or directive. A clear next action makes a focus timer useful.

03

Run a focus or Pomodoro session

Use a timebox that fits the work, protect the session from unrelated items, and stop when the block ends.

04

Review, then choose again

Check the focus history, update the work item, and decide whether to continue, break, or switch tasks.

Keep the timer subordinate to the task.

The calendar explains why the work matters; the timer simply creates a clear boundary for doing it.
